## Повний опис (790/4000)

Can you think like a grandmaster?

In this game you don't play against a computer — you guess the moves that great champions made in real games. Choose Kasparov, Carlsen, Fischer, Tal or any of 18 grandmasters and replay their most famous games move by move.

• 108 real games: from Morphy's 1858 Opera Game to 21st-century world championship matches
• Every move is evaluated by a chess engine: even if you don't match the master but find an equally strong move, you score points
• Profiles of 18 champions: biography, playing style, strengths
• Move of the day — one position a day, the same for everyone
• GM index: how close your thinking is to each grandmaster
• Colour themes, board colours, sound and game pace
• Works offline

Learn to think like the champions — one move at a time.
